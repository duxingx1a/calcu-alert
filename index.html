Population-scale Screening for Urolithiasis Using Ensemble Learning with Non-imaging Biomarkers from Routine Health Check-ups

Stacking ensemble model trained on 799,824 routine health check-up records, using non-imaging biomarkers (blood tests, urinalysis, anthropometry, questionnaires). Internal AUROC 0.7164; external validation AUROC 0.6867–0.6939 across three independent centres. Model-guided CT screening at top-5% risk reduced cost per detected case by 79.3% and number needed to image by 82.6%. Top-1% risk stratification achieved PPV 40.03% — a ~10× increase over baseline prevalence. A low-cost, accessible triage tool for population-scale screening, particularly in resource-constrained settings.
